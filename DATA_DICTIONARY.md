# Weekly Input — Data Dictionary

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Week Start (Mon) | Monday date representing the reporting week |
| SKU | Product identifier; must match Product Setup |
| Product Name | Retrieved from Product Setup |
| Units Sold | Units sold for the SKU during the week |
| Total Sales ($) | Total product sales; includes ad-attributed sales |
| Ad Spend ($) | Advertising spend for the SKU during the week |
| Ad Sales ($) | Sales attributed to advertising; subset of total sales |
| ACoS | Calculated as Ad Spend / Ad Sales |
| Unit COGS ($) | Total product cost based on per-unit COGS |
| Referral Fee ($) | Total referral fees based on per-unit fee |
| Fulfillment Fee ($) | Total fulfillment fees based on per-unit fee |
| Other Variable Cost ($) | Total other variable costs |
| Estimated Profit ($) | Total Sales less ad spend and entered product costs/fees |
| Profit / Unit ($) | Estimated profit divided by units sold |
| Sales / Unit ($) | Total sales divided by units sold |
| Notes | Optional comments about the reporting row |
